About HealthConnect

HealthConnect is a college CEP project designed around the idea of helping communities access online health services. The platform brings basic health information, educational resources and simple health-check tools together in one easy-to-use website.


The goal is to improve awareness of digital healthcare resources and encourage people to seek appropriate professional care when needed.
